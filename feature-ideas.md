# Expense Tracker Feature Ideas

## Analytics & Insights
- Monthly/yearly spending trends and patterns
- Category breakdown with percentage distributions
- Spending vs. income ratio tracking
- Biggest expense days/periods identification
- Average daily/weekly/monthly spending
- Seasonal spending pattern analysis

## Comparisons & Benchmarks
- Month-over-month expense comparison
- Year-over-year growth/reduction in spending
- Compare current month to average of last 6 months
- Weekend vs. weekday spending patterns
- Compare spending across different categories over time

## Budgeting & Goals
- Budget vs. actual spending per category
- Savings goals tracking
- Spending limit alerts and warnings
- Progress toward financial targets

## Advanced Data Views
- Expense frequency analysis (how often you spend in each category)
- Top merchants/vendors by spending
- Expense clustering by amount ranges
- Cash flow visualization (money in vs. money out)
- Recurring expense identification and tracking

## Predictive Features
- Projected monthly spending based on current pace
- End-of-year spending forecasts
- Bill reminder predictions based on past patterns
- Unusual spending detection (outliers)

## Export & Reporting
- Monthly/quarterly expense reports
- Tax-ready category summaries
- Custom date range reports
- CSV/PDF export for accounting software
